A New Home & A Fresh Bloom – Halcyon 17 Calla Lily

October 24, 2026 · 2 min read
Alif Fathur (herobuxx)
Lead Developer

Welcome to the official Halcyon blog! We are thrilled to launch this space as a central hub where we will share news, update logs, and insights into the development of Halcyon.

Along with opening this blog, we are excited to officially announce Halcyon 17, codenamed Calla Lily.

Special Thanks​

Reaching this milestone would not have been possible without the dedication and hard work of several key contributors.

We would like to extend our sincere thanks to:

  • Raya (rktdnt) for taking the initiative to lead the source code bringup.
  • Binzet and Naoko Shoto for creating stunning wallpapers and design assets.
  • Eren for generously covering our hosting infrastructure.
  • neroices for building and designing our new website.
  • Everyone who signed up for our Closed Beta Test. Your enthusiasm and early feedback mean a lot to us.

What is Coming Next?​

We are currently preparing for the upcoming Public Beta release.

Here is a quick look at what you can expect:

  • Feature Parity: Most of your favorite features from Halcyon 16.2 have already been ported over, and any remaining ones will be added soon.
  • Refreshed UI: We have given the user interface a subtle refresh for a cleaner and smoother experience.
  • More to Come: Additional customization and system enhancements are actively in development.

Source Code & Building​

For developers, maintainers, and enthusiasts looking to dive in, the source code for Halcyon 17 is now available and buildable!

If you would like to compile Halcyon for your device or start bringing up a new tree, please refer to our Wiki for complete guides on syncing the manifest, setting up your build environment, and compiling the source.

Stay tuned to this blog for future announcement posts, changelogs, and release schedules. Thank you for being part of the Halcyon community!
