Enchanted by ServerHive

November 4, 2026 · One min read
Alif Fathur (herobuxx)
Lead Developer

We are glad to share that Halcyon has been selected as a grant recipient for the ServerHive Open Source Support Program (Season 2).

Through this program, ServerHive is providing us with compute infrastructure to help power our ongoing development.

Supporting the Open-Source Ecosystem​

Running an open-source Android project requires significant resources, and initiatives like the ServerHive OSS Support Program play a vital role in helping independent teams bring their ideas to life. By providing compute capacity to projects across the custom ROM and Android community, ServerHive is actively helping developers build, maintain, and innovate.

We’re really excited to have ServerHive backing us for this season. Huge thanks to their team for creating this program and stepping up to support open-source Android development.

If you are looking for fast, reliable, and affordable build servers tailored for developers, check out their official links below:

  • Instant Purchases: @ServerHive_Bot
  • Support: @ServerHiveSupport_bot
  • Updates & Announcement Channel: @ServerHive
  • OSS Program: ServerHive GitHub Repository
